Help farmers near you

When Ijisho checks a bean leaf, farmers can send the photo and result to an agronomist in their district on WhatsApp. Register to be one of them.

  • Farmers see your name, role and organisation, and can message you on WhatsApp.
  • You receive a link to the leaf photo with Ijisho’s result. You reply directly to the farmer.
  • Every registration is checked before it appears. You can ask to be removed at any time.
