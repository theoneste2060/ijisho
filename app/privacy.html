Checking a leaf

The leaf check runs entirely on your phone. Your photo is not uploaded when Ijisho checks it, and Ijisho has no accounts, adverts or tracking.

Asking an agronomist

Only if you tap an agronomist’s name, Ijisho uploads a smaller copy of that leaf photo with the result (for example “Bean rust, 95%”) and the district you chose, and creates a private link to it. WhatsApp then opens with a message to that agronomist containing the link. Ijisho does not receive your phone number or name: the agronomist sees them only because you message them on WhatsApp. The photo and its link are deleted automatically after 30 days.

If you use “Share another way”, the photo goes only to the app and person you choose and is not uploaded to Ijisho.

Your chosen district and language are saved only on your phone.

Agronomists who register

Agronomists, extension officers and farmer promoters can register at ijisho-amber.vercel.app/agronomist. We store the name, WhatsApp number, role, organisation, districts, languages and optional note they give. After we check a registration, Ijisho shows the name, role, organisation and WhatsApp number to farmers in the chosen districts, as they agreed when registering. To change or remove a registration, contact us and we do it within 7 days.

Where the data is kept

Registrations and escalation photos are stored on Cloudflare (Workers and D1). The app is served by Vercel. Messages between farmers and agronomists go through WhatsApp under WhatsApp’s own terms; Ijisho does not see them.

Your rights

Under Rwanda’s law on the protection of personal data and privacy (Law N° 058/2021), you can ask to see, correct or delete information about you. Email or WhatsApp us with the number you used; we reply within 30 days.
