Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026 (no data is stored). Ijisho is a free, open-source project run by Theoneste NZAKIZWANIMANA in Kigali, Rwanda. Questions or requests: theonestedev@gmail.com, WhatsApp +250 788 961 647.

Checking a leaf

The leaf check runs entirely on your phone. Your photo is not uploaded anywhere, and Ijisho has no accounts, adverts, tracking or database.

Asking an agronomist

“Ask an agronomist” opens your phone’s share menu with a smaller copy of the photo and the result. The photo goes only to the app and person you choose (for example WhatsApp or SMS), under that app’s own terms. Ijisho does not receive it.

Your language choice is saved only on your phone.

Hosting

The app’s files are served by Vercel. After the first visit they are stored on your phone so Ijisho works offline.

Your rights

Ijisho keeps no personal data about you. For any question under Rwanda’s law on the protection of personal data and privacy (Law N° 058/2021), contact us using the details above.
